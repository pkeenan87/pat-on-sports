**Result:** UCLA 17, Wisconsin 7 at Rose Bowl Stadium in Pasadena, Saturday, Oct. 17, 2026. The Bruins move to 6-0 (4-0 Big Ten).
After a huge win for the team over ranked Oregon, I was very concerned about this game. It was the classic trap game against a team that is built to upset better teams. The badgers played tough and refused to let the UCLA win this game by running the ball. The bruins proved they can win a game without dominating on the ground and without Nico at QB. The schedule starts to get easier now and the sky is the limit for this team. Now can some UCLA fans start showing up to the games? What more does this team need to do? We literally have people flying from the east coast to support bob and fill these empty seats! These are salt of the earth people who love football and love winning! What is going on in Los Angeles that is so great that they can’t go watch a top team in the country at the Rose Bowl????
## The Passing Game
I loved that we can throw the ball a little more now, especially since it means Landon Ellis will get more of an opportunity to affect winning. He was a monster last year at JMU and hasn’t had enough opportunities so far this year. He was able to haul in two big catches and I expect he will enjoy playing with Ty at QB. While I enjoyed Ty throwing the ball and moving the chains, I was frustrated with the emphasis on the passing in the red zone. Let’s not forget what got us here! Run that ball and let’s get the ball in the endzone behind the hogs we have up front. I give Wisconsin credit for a great defensive game plan but I would have liked to see UCLA stick with the run a little more. 
## The Defense
Colton Joseph was held to less than 100 yards passing and the offense as a whole only put up 7 points. On a day where the offense struggled, the defense really carried the day and ensured that the bruins stayed unbeaten. There were multiple moments in this game where I was concerned that the momentum was swinging to Wisconsin and the defense stepped up every single time. Jalen Woods had a great game, it seemed like he was all over the field. 
## Looking Forward to what’s next
This is a light blog but there really wasn’t much to this game. It was a win but I thought UCLA underachieved in the game. You could tell by Chesney’s post game interview, yet it is wonderful that they are 6-0, yes it’s a great story that Ty went from Pitt to Cal Poly to now quarterbacking the 6-0 UCLA Bruins around the corner from where he grew up, but this was not a good performance. If a couple plays go differently, the bruins could certainly have lost this game. It’s also true that the bruins could play like this for the next three to four games and win. Their schedule is easy, they are the toast of every college football show and at this point they have a decision to make. Do they get complacent, soak in all of the good press and let their egos grow and grow or do they double their efforts, focus on improving and make a real run at a national championship this year. After watching some of the other top teams this weekend there is no reason UCLA can’t be in the final four or even the national championship game. How they approach this next month of football will determine where they will go in the following month. Regardless, a win is a win and I can’t believe they are 6-0. Pride in Pads!
