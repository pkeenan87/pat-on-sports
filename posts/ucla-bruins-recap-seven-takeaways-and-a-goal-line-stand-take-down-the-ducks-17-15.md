**Result:** No. 21 UCLA 17, No. 13 Oregon 15 at Autzen Stadium in Eugene, Saturday, Oct. 10, 2026. The Bruins move to 5-0 (3-0 Big Ten), and it's their first win at Oregon since 2004.
Two weeks ago I said the Oregon game could put this team on the map, and that's what happened, even if it was ugly. This game was a battle to see who could “not lose” and the bruins were able to manage their mistakes enough to take advantage of a horrific performance by Dylan Raiola. 
## The Defense Won This Game
Seven takeaways. 
- Tyson Ford recovered a Jordon Davison fumble at the UCLA 15 to end an Oregon drive in the second quarter.
- Anthony Sacca strip-sacked Dylan Raiola, and Amier Washington recovered. Four plays later Knight scored.
- Ta'Shawn James picked off Raiola with about a minute left in the first half. That's his fourth of the season, which leads the nation.
- Scooter Jackson forced an Evan Stewart fumble in the third quarter and Jalen Woods recovered.
- Tao Johnson intercepted Raiola late in the third, and that set up the Knight touchdown catch.
- Sacca again: he recovered Brock Thomas' fumble on a fourth-and-1 early in the fourth.
- Dante Lovett recovered the fumble on the final lateral play to end the game.
## The Goal Line Stand
This is what I'll remember. Thomas hit Stewart for a 44-yard touchdown with 2:16 left to make it 17-15. Thomas got stuffed on the two-point try, but UCLA was called for a face mask and Oregon got another shot from about a half yard out. The defensive line stopped Jordon Davison short. Oregon also failed a two-point try after its first-quarter touchdown, and those two failures were the difference. Then on the last play, Thomas hit Stewart, Stewart lateraled to Jamari Johnson, the next lateral got broken up and Lovett recovered it. Game over.
## Wayne Knight and the Running Game
22 carries for 103 yards, a 35-yard touchdown on a direct snap in the second quarter (I feel like a touchdown run like this happens every single game), and a 9-yard touchdown catch from Ty Dieffenbach on third-and-goal early in the fourth to make it 17-6. This wasn't the 277-yard kind of rushing day we saw against Cal. UCLA ran 47 times for 140 yards (3.0 per carry) against a very good Oregon front. But the Bruins held the ball for 39:15, and that's how you protect a defense.
## Nico, Ty and the Quarterback Situation
Nico started hot, hitting Aidan Mizell for 62 yards on the very first play from scrimmage. He finished 10 of 17 for 162 yards with no touchdowns and two interceptions. The second pick was a throw into the end zone right before halftime, and the end of the first half was rough overall: a blocked field goal and two interceptions in the last few minutes. One thing I know about Chesney is he hates turnovers, Nico not being able to finish the game allowed them to switch gears and reduce the risk in the gameplan. 
Ty Dieffenbach came in instead of Madden Iamaleava (Madden had been the backup all season) and went 3 of 6 for 43 yards and the touchdown to Knight. He mostly managed the game and let the run game and defense do the work.
