**Result:** Patriots 20, Steelers 3 — home opener at Gillette Stadium, Sunday, Sept. 20, 2026. Patriots move to 1-1.
## Introduction
Not pretty, but it counts. The defense held Pittsburgh to one field goal, sacked Aaron Rodgers four times and scored a touchdown of its own. The offense? 13 points: a 39-yard TreVeyon Henderson touchdown run and two field goals. With A.J. Brown out with the ankle, the offense survived on a couple of big plays and the defense did the rest. I'll take the win and still be a little worried.
## Pros
- The defense - this was the story. Rodgers went 23 of 39 for 187 yards with an interception, got sacked four times and fumbled twice. Pittsburgh's longest play of the day was a 26-yard catch-and-run by Jaylen Warren. Holding a team to three points is about as good as it gets.
- Christian Barmore and Elijah Ponder - up 13-3 in the fourth, Barmore punched the ball out on a strip-sack and Ponder scooped it and went 19 yards for the touchdown. That one ended it.
- TreVeyon Henderson - season debut, 16 carries, 76 yards and the 39-yard touchdown run in the first quarter. He came in for Stevenson after the fumble, found a crease and was gone. He looked like the best runner on the field.
- Christian Gonzalez on DK Metcalf - Gonzalez shadowed Metcalf at times and Metcalf finished with four catches for 27 yards. Rodgers had to look elsewhere all day.
- Romeo Doubs bounced back - after a zero-catch, big-drop debut in Seattle, he had three catches for 96 yards, including a 63-yarder in the third quarter that set up Andy Borregales' second field goal and a 29-yarder on a scramble drill. Good for him.
- Marcus Jones - a 19-yard punt return set up the Henderson touchdown drive.
## Cons
- The offense is still not right - one offensive touchdown and two field goals at home. If the defense doesn't score, this is a 13-3 game against a team that struggled to move the ball all day. That's not going to cut it every week.
- Drake Maye - 14 of 22 for 208 yards, no touchdowns and another interception (Patrick Queen). Better than Seattle, but the big passing numbers were mostly the two Doubs plays. He's still not playing like last year's guy.
- Rhamondre Stevenson's fumble - the opening drive got to the Pittsburgh 34 and then Nick Herbig stripped him. He lost two fumbles against Pittsburgh last season too. That's how Henderson got the job for the rest of the day.
- Third downs - 4 for 12. Not good enough.
- Jalen Ramsey's taunting penalty on the Stevenson fumble pushed Pittsburgh back to its own 16, which was nice for us, but it's still the kind of thing that hurts you in a close game.
- No A.J. Brown - the passing game without him leans hard on a couple of explosive plays. Hollins, Doubs and Douglas took most of the receiver snaps. Somebody needs to step up while he's out.
