**Result:** Jaguars 35, Patriots 6 — on the road at EverBank Stadium in Jacksonville, Sunday, Sept. 27, 2026. Patriots fall to 1-2.
## Introduction
This one was over by the third quarter. Drake Maye turned it over three times (two interceptions and a lost fumble), Trevor Lawrence threw three touchdowns, and Mike Vrabel pulled Maye for Tommy DeVito in the fourth with the game out of reach. The Patriots never scored a touchdown. While this was really ugly and sickening to watch, this usually happens to the Patriots when they play in Florida in September. It is way too hot and you get some ugly performances. 
## Pros
- Marcus Jones - picked off a Lawrence pass that deflected off Brenton Strange's hands and returned it to the Jacksonville 21. It led to a field goal, which, in this game, counted as a highlight.
- Mack Hollins - six catches for 87 yards. He keeps being the most reliable guy out there, which is a pro for Hollins and maybe a con for everybody else.
- The Efton Chism III play - Maye threw a backward pass to Chism, who dodged a tackle at the sideline and threw to Hollins for 16 yards. Ugly, tipped, and it worked. It led to a field goal.
- The defense kept it close for a half - it was 14-3 at halftime, with Jacksonville's first touchdown coming off a Maye fumble. It wasn't the defense's day, but the first half didn't fall apart on them.
- Andy Borregales - he hit from 35 and 40. He was the entire scoring offense.
## Cons
- Drake Maye - 14 of 25 for 199 yards, no touchdowns, two interceptions, a lost fumble, sacked three times, and a 48.6 passer rating, the second-lowest of his career. Through three games he has one touchdown pass and six interceptions, and seven turnovers. He threw eight picks all of last season. I said after Seattle that he needs to win with his brain. That hasn't happened yet.
- The fumble - the ball slipped out of his hand during his throwing motion with no one around him. Jacksonville recovered and went 62 yards for the first touchdown.
- Third-and-goal from the 2 - Maye rolled right and threw it into coverage in the end zone, and Travis Hunter got his first career interception. That took points off the board right before halftime and kept it 14-3.
- The Jarrian Jones interception - Maye threw it right to him to open a drive. Jacksonville scored on a 5-yard Rodriguez run on the very next play, and it was 28-3 after three.
- No running game - 26 carries for 83 yards. Henderson had 8 for 23 and Stevenson 7 for 22. When you can't run, everything falls on Maye, and that went how it went.
- Third downs and penalties - 4 for 12 on third down again, and 9 penalties for 68 yards. Jacksonville also had a 15-play, 72-yard drive that ate 8:42 in the third quarter. The defense got worn down.
