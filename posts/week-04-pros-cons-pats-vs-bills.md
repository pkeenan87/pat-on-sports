**Result:** Patriots 29, Bills 26, on the road at the new Highmark Stadium in Orchard Park, Sunday, Oct. 4, 2026. Patriots move to 2-2. Buffalo takes its first loss and drops to 3-1.
## Introduction
Now that's more like it. A week after Jacksonville, the Patriots went into Buffalo as 7-point underdogs and won it on a one-handed Efton Chism III touchdown with 1:50 left. Drake Maye went 22 of 37 for 269 yards and three touchdowns, and he led the team in rushing with 54 yards on eight carries. The offense scored four touchdowns, twice as many as it had in the first three games combined. Craig Woodson picked off Josh Allen to close it out. It wasn't clean (two more Maye turnovers and a messy end to the first half), but I said he needs to win with his brain, and in the fourth quarter he did. 
## Pros
- Drake Maye - 22 of 37, 269 yards, 3 TDs, plus 54 rushing yards. Coming into the game he had one touchdown pass and six interceptions. He was pressured a career-high 21 times and still made plays. On the game-winning drive he went 4 of 7 for 68 yards. That's the guy from last year.
- Efton Chism III - he only played big snaps because Mack Hollins left with a calf injury in the third quarter. He had a 19-yard catch on the final drive, then the 23-yard one-handed touchdown on a Maye rollout. That is one of the best catches I have ever seen
- Romeo Doubs - 6 catches, 58 yards, 2 TDs (the 14-yarder and a short goal-line fade in the third quarter for a 21-17 lead). After the zero-catch debut in Seattle, he's trending the right way.
- Craig Woodson - Dre'Mont Jones got pressure on a stunt, Allen threw it deep for Joshua Palmer, and Woodson high-pointed it at the 19 with 1:14 left. Ballgame.
- Hunter Henry - 5 catches for 62 yards, including the 12-yarder on third-and-10 in the final minute that let us kneel it out. He has been absent for weeks and it was nice to have him back
- Elijah Ponder - a sack of Allen and the fumble recovery when Robert Spillane knocked the ball out of Khalil Shakir's hands right before half. Spillane had a team-high 8 tackles.
## Cons
- Turnovers again - Maye got strip-sacked in the second quarter (T.J. Parker and Ed Oliver split the sack, Greg Gaines recovered), and right after Ponder recovered the Shakir fumble, Maye threw an interception to Dee Alford on the next play. Alford returned it 37 yards and Tyler Bass kicked a 27-yarder at the gun. That turned a possible lead into 17-14 Bills at halftime.
- Pass protection - Maye was pressured 21 times. Will Campbell allowed six pressures, and he also had a holding call that wiped out a 10-yard Maye scramble in the fourth quarter. 
- Run defense - James Cook averaged 6.3 yards a carry (12 for 75 and a TD), and Buffalo probably should have given him the ball more.
- Injuries - already without A.J. Brown (high ankle sprain), Christian Gonzalez and Christian Barmore (both shoulder), and then Hollins went down with the calf.
