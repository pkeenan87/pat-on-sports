**Result:** Patriots 45, Raiders 17, at home at Gillette Stadium in Foxborough, Sunday, Oct. 11, 2026. Patriots move to 3-2 (2-0 at home). Las Vegas drops to 3-2.
## Introduction
The Raiders came into Foxborough at 3-1 and left with a 28-point loss. Drake Maye went 26 of 34 for 374 yards and four touchdowns with no interceptions, and ran one in himself. In my opinion, this is the best game of Drake Maye’s career. He could not miss a pass and was unstoppable when running. Wow.
## Pros
- Drake Maye - 26 of 34, 374 yards, 4 TDs, 0 INTs, sacked once. He added 25 rushing yards on seven carries and a 3-yard touchdown run in the third quarter. 
- DeMario Douglas - 4 catches, 65 yards, 2 TDs: a 35-yarder for the opening score in the first quarter and an 8-yarder to finish the scoring in the fourth.
- Kyle Williams - 3 catches for 91 yards, including a 34-yard touchdown in the third quarter that made it 28-7. I am not sure about Williams long term in the NFL but today he looked like DeSean Jackson
- Elijah Ponder - 2 sacks of Kirk Cousins and 6 tackles. The defense had 3 sacks total. This guy has been a monster signing
- Run defense - Las Vegas ran 18 times for 38 yards (2.1 a carry). Ashton Jeanty had 27 yards on 10 carries. Is Jeanty a bust?
## Cons
- Brock Bowers - 8 catches for 110 yards, including a 13-yard touchdown that tied it 7-7 in the first quarter. Bowers is the best tight end in the NFL so I don’t mind it too much but we couldn’t do anything with him
- Penalties - 12 for 116 yards. That's a lot to give away in a game you win by 28.
- Injuries - left tackle Will Campbell (knee) and running back TreVeyon Henderson (ankle) both got hurt in the first half and were ruled out for the rest of the game. These injuries are becoming a giant problem
