**Result:** Patriots 26, Jets 19, at home at Gillette Stadium in Foxborough, Sunday, Oct. 18, 2026. Patriots move to 4-2 (3-0 at home). Jets drop to 2-4.
## Introduction
This was a brutal game, the Jets played tough and I am sure glad that it is over and we won the game. If that game lasted another quarter, we may not have any offensive linemen left to finish the game! This goes to show that no matter how much of a gap you perceive there is between two teams, anything can happen on Sunday especially when it is division rivals 
## Pros
- Drake Maye - continued his momentum from last week. I think he looked good when throwing the football and did not get much help this week as there were multiple bad drops (mostly by AJ Brown)
- Hunter Henry - He had multiple big catches to convert on third and fourth down and the touchdown that made us all breathe a sigh of relief. I am very happy that Henry is back in the mix for the Pats offense
- Corey Kiner was a battering ram, I love running backs that run straight. His work on the last drive of the game to keep the chains moving and run the clock was much appreciated
- Pats defense played super tough. Ponder did not have as big of a game as last week but I noticed him pressuring the QB on big downs. He has been an excellent signing
- The offensive line, or what is left of them. Maye had all day to throw on that Doubs touchdown and was not pressured much during the game. They also blocked great for the 4 minute drill at the end of the game which pretty much sealed the victory
## Cons
- Injuries, when will it ever stop! Once I saw Ben Brown limping off of the field I felt sick. I can’t take any more injuries and I need it to stop at some point. 
- Turnovers. The Demario Douglas fumble is a classic case of doing too much. The turnovers are so bad and we are so lucky this Jets team isn’t a little bit better otherwise they could have won this game due to our turnovers
