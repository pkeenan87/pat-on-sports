**Result:** Bears 30, Patriots 24, on the road at Soldier Field in Chicago, Thursday, Oct. 22, 2026. Patriots fall to 4-3. Bears improve to 5-2.
## Introduction
I hate Thursday Night Football games, I hate them so much I want them banned. They are typically very sloppy, low scoring, and painful to watch. This game wasn’t low scoring and it wasn’t the typical boring Thursday game but I hated it just as much as I always do. The Patriots have shown me who they are this year, they are a talented team who can play with anyone but too stupid to be considered a good team. This is the opposite of what good teams do, which is NOT beat themselves. If the Patriots are good at one thing this year, it is beating themselves and I am sick of it.
## Pros
- Drake Maye had a good game on paper - 24/31 for 321 is really good. Maye could have thrown for closer to 400 if some of the receivers could have brought in the deep balls. While Maye is still not perfect, he seems to have corrected the funk he was in the first four games
- Hunter Henry is officially back. He was their most reliable target the entire night and I like the steadiness he brings to this team (no one else is steady). Eli Raridon also looks better every week. If this team can ever fix their stupidity problem and get healthy, this TE duo could be a problem in December when it isn’t as easy to chuck the ball around
- The offensive line - the pass blocking issues have basically been corrected this year. If you had told me the pass blocking would have been this good, I never would have thought our record would be 4-3.
## Cons
- Stupidity. In some ways I think it is a miracle we were ever in this game. The lucky TD from Stevenson flipped the momentum back around before the half when things were looking hopeless at 20-7. In other ways I feel like we completely gave this game away. Maye only had one INT but it was in the red zone again. These red zone turnovers are total back breakers and that was an awful throw. Maye has tons of talent and I am glad he is our QB but he sucks at risk management
- Since we gave up 30 points and 130 yards (including a 58 yard catch) to Luther Burden on a blown coverage I have to talk about the defense. We had chances to win this game but we could not come up with the stops when we needed them. I do have to mention that Caleb Williams was on fire and made some incredible throws. I haven’t been a believer in Caleb Williams but I may have to change my mind on him. He refused to lose that game
- More stupid penalties. The Pats had 10 penalties for 71 yards including one on 4th and 2 that looked like we would convert easily with a pass to Hunter Henry. How is a team that went to the Super Bowl a year ago this stupid?
